2. 过敏性鼻炎和非过敏性鼻炎有什么区别？

患者易懂版

非过敏性鼻炎患者的症状常和过敏性鼻炎相似，但他们较少说打喷嚏和痒，更多说鼻塞更重、流鼻涕、鼻窦性头痛和鼻涕倒流。

Patients with NAR often exhibit similar symptoms to AR patients but complain less of sneezing and itching and more of worsening nasal congestion, rhinorrhea, sinus headaches, and post-nasal drip [6,7].
来源：Allergic and Mixed Rhinitis: Diagnosis and Natural Evolution (2019 full-text selected sections)

资料解释

非过敏性鼻炎（NAR）患者的症状常与过敏性鼻炎（AR）相似，但较少主诉打喷嚏和瘙痒，更多报告鼻塞加重、流涕、鼻窦性头痛及鼻后滴漏。

Patients with NAR often exhibit similar symptoms to AR patients but complain less of sneezing and itching and more of worsening nasal congestion, rhinorrhea, sinus headaches, and post-nasal drip [6,7].
来源：Allergic and Mixed Rhinitis: Diagnosis and Natural Evolution (2019 full-text selected sections)

过敏性鼻炎（AR）的确认需要皮肤点刺或血清sIgE检测阳性且与暴露于致敏吸入性过敏原后的症状相关，而非过敏性鼻炎（NAR）的诊断是在吸入性过敏原皮肤点刺或血清sIgE检测阴性后作出。

Whereas confirmation of AR requires positive skin-prick or serologic sIgE testing that correlates with symptoms after exposure to the sensitizing aeroallergen, a diagnosis of NAR is made after negative skin-prick or serum sIgE testing to aeroallergens.
来源：Allergic and Mixed Rhinitis: Diagnosis and Natural Evolution (2019 full-text selected sections)

非过敏性鼻炎（NAR）患者的诱因包括刺激物（香烟烟雾、香水、清洁产品）、天气变化（气压、温度）及其他强烈气味。

Triggers for NAR patients include irritants (cigarette smoke, perfumes, cleaning products), weather changes (barometric pressure, temperature), and other strong odors.
来源：Allergic and Mixed Rhinitis: Diagnosis and Natural Evolution (2019 full-text selected sections)
